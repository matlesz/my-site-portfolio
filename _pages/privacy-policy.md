This website is operated by Mat Leszkiewicz. For privacy questions, use the [Contact page](/contact/). It links to the available contact channels without publishing an email address here.

## Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 only if you select **Accept analytics** in the consent banner. Analytics is optional and is not loaded before consent. If you reject analytics, the site does not load the Google Analytics script or create Google Analytics cookies.

When enabled, Google Analytics may process information such as page views, approximate location, device and browser information, and online identifiers. It is used to understand how visitors use the site and improve its content. Google acts as the analytics service provider and may process data outside the European Economic Area under its applicable transfer arrangements.

You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer. Withdrawing consent prevents future analytics loading after the page is reloaded and removes analytics cookies that can be removed by this site. It does not erase information already sent to Google; requests concerning that information should be directed through the applicable Google controls.

The consent choice is stored locally in your browser so the site can remember it. If browser storage is unavailable, the site treats the visitor as having made no choice and keeps analytics disabled.

## Fonts and external services

The site’s Manrope and Space Grotesk fonts are served from this website’s own assets. The site does not request those fonts from Google. If you choose to enable analytics, the Google Analytics script is requested from Google as described above.
